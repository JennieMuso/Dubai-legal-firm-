# Design direction

The visual idea is **clear counsel, in context**. The homepage moves between white editorial space and deep-green legal chapters, pairing every claim with a document, a place or people doing the work.

The palette is deep forest `#063A2D`, dark forest `#02261D`, green `#0F5B44`, soft mint `#DFECE6`, mist `#F0F5F2`, white and black text. Lime is not part of the system.

The composition follows an editorial legal-services rhythm: a full-bleed first screen, a compact proof band, an asymmetrical answer section, a dark practice-area chapter with tall equal-height image-overlay cards, a split process sequence, a two-row grid of extended lawyer portraits, a people-led approach collage, a place-led statement, a four-card testimonial carousel, concise questions and a full-bleed photographic close.

Display type is compact and left aligned. The desktop H1 tops out at 80px, section titles at 64px and supporting headings below that. Components use square corners, flat colour, no resting shadows, glass effects or decorative rules. Neutral black gradients are reserved for image-legibility scrims that stay clear through the centre and blend into the lower text area without tinting the photography. Section spacing stays within a tighter five-to-seven-rem rhythm.

Motion uses native scroll with GSAP as progressive enhancement. Images drift subtly, proof figures count once, practice-area imagery rises into view, testimonial controls use native scroll snapping, and FAQ panels animate their measured height in both directions. Reduced-motion users receive the complete static composition.
