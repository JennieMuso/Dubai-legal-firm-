# Design direction

The visual idea is **clear counsel, in context**. The homepage moves between white editorial space and deep-green legal chapters, pairing every claim with a document, a place or people doing the work.

The palette is deep forest `#063A2D`, dark forest `#02261D`, green `#0F5B44`, soft mint `#DFECE6`, mist `#F0F5F2`, white and black text. Lime is not part of the system.

The homepage follows an editorial legal-services rhythm: a full-bleed first screen, a compact proof band, an asymmetrical answer section, a dark practice-area chapter, a split process sequence, a two-row grid of extended lawyer portraits, a people-led approach collage, a place-led statement, a four-card testimonial carousel, concise questions and a full-bleed photographic close. The practice-area chapter uses a 12-column bento instead of a supporting image panel: the lead card spans seven columns and two rows, two secondary cards stack beside it, and a wide fourth card closes the composition. The hierarchy collapses to paired cards on tablet and a uniform single column on mobile.

The inner pages share a full-bleed photographic hero, persistent navigation and the same closing consultation panel and footer, then establish their own editorial structure. About combines a positioning bento, long-form narrative, principles table and connected-matter grid. How We Work uses a numbered decision path, matter-specific bento and communication framework. FAQ groups twelve questions by user intent, pairs them with a sticky category index and closes with a preparation bento. These layouts keep the system recognisable without making every page repeat the homepage.

Display type is compact and left aligned. The desktop H1 tops out at 80px, section titles at 64px and supporting headings below that. Components use square corners, flat colour, no resting shadows, glass effects or decorative rules. Neutral black gradients are reserved for image-legibility scrims that stay clear through the centre and blend into the lower text area without tinting the photography. Section spacing stays within a tighter five-to-seven-rem rhythm.

Motion uses native scroll with GSAP as progressive enhancement. Images drift subtly, proof figures count once, practice-area tiles and inner-page bento cells reveal in sequence, large text clips upward as it enters the viewport, and process steps translate gently with the reading direction. Testimonial controls use native scroll snapping, FAQ panels animate their measured height in both directions, and the active FAQ category follows the reader. Reduced-motion users receive the complete static composition.
