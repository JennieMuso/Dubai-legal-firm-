# Design direction

The visual idea is **clear counsel, in context**. The homepage moves between white editorial space and deep-green legal chapters, pairing every claim with a document, a place or people doing the work.

The palette is deep forest `#063A2D`, dark forest `#02261D`, green `#0F5B44`, soft mint `#DFECE6`, mist `#F0F5F2`, white and black text. Lime is not part of the system.

The composition follows an editorial legal-services rhythm: a full-bleed first screen, a compact proof band, an asymmetrical answer section, a dark services chapter with image-led vertical cards, a split process sequence, an offset lawyer grid, a people-led approach collage, a place-led statement, concise questions and a decisive green close.

Display type is compact and left aligned. The desktop H1 tops out at 80px, section titles at 64px and supporting headings below that. Components use square corners, flat colour and no gradients, resting shadows, glass effects or decorative rules. Section spacing stays within a tighter five-to-seven-rem rhythm.

Motion uses native scroll with GSAP as progressive enhancement. Images drift subtly, proof figures count once, service imagery rises into view, and vertical cards recede as the next area takes focus. Reduced-motion users receive the complete static composition.
