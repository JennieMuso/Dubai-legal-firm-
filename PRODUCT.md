# Bayline Legal website concept

Bayline Legal is a working placeholder identity for a Dubai Business Bay law firm. The website must help founders, companies, investors, and private clients understand the practice quickly, see how the firm approaches a matter, find direct answers to common Dubai business-law questions, and start a confidential conversation. The current concept includes the homepage, a Practice Areas overview with four detailed practice pages, and About, How We Work and FAQ pages.

The site is a client showcase. Firm name, lawyer names and biographies, qualifications, awards, address, phone number, proof figures, testimonial wording, and regulated claims are illustrative and must be replaced with verified, approved facts before a production launch.

The primary action is an email consultation request. The site must feel calm under pressure, precise, locally grounded, useful on mobile, and structured for search and answer engines without unsupported claims.
