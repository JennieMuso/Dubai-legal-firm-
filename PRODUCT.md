# Bayline Legal homepage

Bayline Legal is a working placeholder identity for a Dubai Business Bay law firm. The homepage must help founders, companies, investors, and private clients understand the practice quickly, find direct answers to common Dubai business-law questions, and start a confidential conversation.

The page is a first-round concept. Firm name, lawyer names and biographies, credentials, address, phone number, proof figures, testimonial wording, and regulated claims must be replaced with approved facts before a production launch.

The primary action is an email consultation request. The site must feel calm under pressure, precise, locally grounded, useful on mobile, and structured for search and answer engines without unsupported claims.
