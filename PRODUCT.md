# Bayline Legal homepage

Bayline Legal is a working placeholder identity for a Dubai Business Bay law firm. The homepage must help founders, companies, investors, and private clients understand the practice quickly and start a confidential conversation.

The page is a first-round concept. Firm name, lawyer biographies, credentials, address, phone number, and regulated claims must be replaced with approved facts before a production launch.

The primary action is an email consultation request. The site must feel calm under pressure, precise, locally grounded, and useful on mobile.
